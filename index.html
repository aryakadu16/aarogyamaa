                Support Around You

                Recovery is easier when it isn't done alone. Family and caregivers can help by:

                Meals

                Cook simple, nourishing food and keep water within reach.

                Rest

                Protect naps. Take the baby for a stretch so she can sleep.

                Baby care

                Handle diapers, burping and settling between feeds.

                Household

                Laundry, dishes and visitors are theirs to manage.

                Appointments

                Keep track of visits and vaccinations, and go along.

                Emotional support

                Listen without fixing. Notice if low mood lingers and encourage help.
